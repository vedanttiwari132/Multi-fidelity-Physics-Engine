KNOCKOUTPHYSICS MMA

First-person, physics-driven MMA in the octagon. Every strike is a real collision, so speed, range and timing decide the damage. Watch your consciousness (gold bar): head shots drain it and the lower it gets, the less steady you are. Kicks wreck balance and can sweep a fighter off his feet. The limb he's about to throw glows, and the ring around your sights shows where it's coming from.
